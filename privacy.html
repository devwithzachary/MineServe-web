2. The MineServe App (Local Hosting)

The core MineServe app on Android, Linux, Windows, macOS, and Headless WebUI is 100% open-source (GPL-3.0) and runs locally on your own device without telemetry, ads, or mandatory accounts.

  • No Analytics or Ad SDKs: The app does not contain advertising networks, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs.
  • On-Device Server Data: Your world saves, server configs, plugins/mods, Web Admin credentials, and logs remain strictly on your device unless you configure WebDAV, Discord webhooks, or MineServe Cloud.

3. Optional MineServe Cloud Account Data

Only if you choose to sign in to MineServe Cloud, we collect the minimal data required to operate your account:

  • Email Address: Used for magic sign-in links, 6-digit email verification codes, and linking your self-hosted servers.
  • Linked Devices & Session Tokens: Device/browser labels and revocable tokens so you can unlink sessions anytime.
  • Billing & Subscription Metadata: Managed by Stripe; we store your Stripe Customer ID, tier, and interval, never full card numbers.
  • Custom Subdomains & Cloud Backups: Claimed *.play.mineserve.app / *.playms.uk domains, relay regions, and encrypted .zip backups in Cloudflare R2.

4. Infrastructure, Subprocessors & International Transfers

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Serverless API, DynamoDB metadata, and Amazon SES transactional sign-in emails.
  • Cloudflare (R2, DNS & Pages): Encrypted backup object storage, DNS, and portal hosting.
  • Stripe: Payment processing, invoicing, and subscription portal.
  • Regional Relay Nodes: Forward game and Web Admin traffic without inspecting gameplay contents.

5. Website Analytics

mineserve.app and cloud.mineserve.app use self-hosted, cookieless Umami Analytics (stats.devwithzachary.com, operated by ZEDCEE LTD) under Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)) to measure aggregate page views, download clicks, and basic UI interactions without cookies, cross-site tracking, or personal identifiers.

6. Retention, Your Rights & Contact

  • Retention: Delete backups, release subdomains, or revoke devices anytime. Upon permanent account deletion, cloud account data and backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Your UK GDPR Rights: Access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data by emailing devwithzachary@pm.me.
  • Supervisory Authority: UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
