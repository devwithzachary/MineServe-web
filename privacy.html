Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026 · App & Optional MineServe Cloud

1. Local-First Principle (MineServe Android App)

The MineServe Android application (com.devwithzachary.mineserve) is 100% open source (GPL-3.0), ad-free, and built to run locally on your device. If you use MineServe strictly for local hosting without linking an optional MineServe Cloud account:

  • No account or telemetry required: We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising IDs inside the Android app.
  • On-device storage: World saves, plugins, server configs, and local logs remain exclusively inside your device's private app storage unless you choose to export them.
  • Third-party services you choose: When you download server software (Mojang, PaperMC, Purpur, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Modrinth), use third-party tunnels (Bore, Playit.gg), sync to your own WebDAV/Nextcloud server, or send Discord webhooks, your device communicates directly with those services under their respective privacy policies.

2. Optional MineServe Cloud Account Data

If you choose to sign in to MineServe Cloud (cloud.mineserve.app) for optional custom relay addresses or off-device cloud backups, we collect only the minimum data required to operate the service:

  • Email Address: Used solely to send one-click passwordless magic sign-in links (via Amazon SES), identify your account, and link your MineServe Android app or Web Portal session.
  • Linked Devices & Session Tokens: We store hashed/signed authentication tokens and basic device labels (e.g., device model or browser) so you can view and revoke linked sessions at any time from the Cloud Portal.
  • Subscription & Billing Records: Paid subscriptions (MineServe Plus and MineServe Pro) are processed securely by Stripe. We store your Stripe Customer ID, active plan tier, and subscription status. We never receive or store your full credit/debit card numbers.
  • Server Address & Tunnel Metadata: When you claim a custom *.play.mineserve.app or *.playms.uk subdomain, we store the chosen subdomain, target region, and tunnel routing token so our relay servers can forward traffic to your device.
  • Cloud Backup Files: World and server backup archives you upload are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage along with file name, size, timestamp, and associated server ID so you can list, download, restore, or delete them.

3. Infrastructure & Subprocessors

MineServe Cloud relies on the following infrastructure providers to deliver authentication, billing, relays, and storage:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Serverless API, DynamoDB account/backup metadata, and Amazon SES for transactional sign-in emails.
  • Cloudflare (R2 & DNS/Pages): Object storage for uploaded backup archives and DNS/portal hosting.
  • Stripe: Payment processing, invoicing, and subscription management.
  • Regional Relay Nodes: TCP/HTTP relay servers that forward game and Web Admin traffic between players and your Android device without inspecting gameplay contents.

4. Website Analytics

This marketing website (mineserve.app) uses privacy-friendly, cookieless Umami Analytics to measure aggregate page views and download button clicks without tracking individuals across sites or storing personal cookies.

5. Data Retention, Deletion & Contact

You may delete individual cloud backups, release subdomains, or unlink devices at any time from the Cloud Portal or the MineServe Android app. If you cancel your subscription or wish to delete your MineServe Cloud account and all associated backup data permanently, contact us at support@mineserve.app.
